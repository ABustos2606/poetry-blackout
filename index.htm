How this works

Every day at midnight, a bot performs the following steps:

  1. It asks Gutendex (a public search API for Project Gutenberg) for a random English-language book.
  2. The book's full text is downloaded, the legal boilerplate is trimmed off, and 250 consecutive words are cropped.
  3. Those 250 words are sent to Gemini, which is asked to choose a few of them that read as a short poem, kept in their original order.
  4. The resulting poem is saved and displayed here. Every other word is inked over — still there, holding the page together, just unreadable.
Books via Project Gutenberg (catalog by Gutendex). Poems via your own Gemini key. Not affiliated with either.
